\subsection{Comparison with the convergence claim for SignMuon}\label{app:mishra}

Concurrently, \citet{mishra2026signmuon} introduced the same sign-after-LMO
method under the name \emph{Sign-Muon}, with update direction
$\mathbf{S}_t=\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{M}_t))$ rescaled
to $\mathbf{D}_t=\mathbf{S}_t/\sqrt{mn}$; this coincides with the SignMuon step
of Section~\ref{sec:theory} after absorbing $1/\sqrt{mn}$ into $\eta$. For this
method they establish an $\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{T})$ stationarity guarantee. We
show that this guarantee does not contradict Theorem~\ref{th:1}: the finalized
rate is proved for a different update direction, and for $\mathbf{D}_t$ their
bound is uninformative on the instance of Theorem~\ref{th:1}.

Their analysis controls the stationarity proxy
$\mathcal{G}_T=\tfrac1T\sum_{t}\mathbb{E}[\|\mathbf{g}_t\|_1/\sqrt{mn}]$, where
$\mathbf{g}_t=\nabla f(\mathbf{W}_t)$, through the per-entry sign-error
probabilities $q_{ij,t}=\Pr(\mathbf{S}_{t,ij}\neq
\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{g}_{t,ij})\mid\mathbf{W}_t)$, in two steps. First,
for an arbitrary sign oracle $\mathbf{S}_t$ they derive
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mishra_generic}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{G}_T
&\le
\frac{f(\mathbf{W}_0)-f^\ast}{\eta T}
+\frac{L_\ast}{2}\,\eta\\
&\quad+\underbrace{\frac{2}{T}\sum_{t}\mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{mn}}\sum_{i,j}|\mathbf{g}_{t,ij}|\,q_{ij,t}\right]}_{=:\,R_T}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Inequality~\eqref{eq:mishra_generic} applies in particular to
$\mathbf{S}_t=\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{M}_t))$, but with
the residual $R_T$ left unbounded. Second, the rate is obtained by estimating
$R_T$, and this estimate is derived \emph{only} for the gradient-sign oracle
$\mathbf{S}_t=\operatorname{sign}(\widetilde{\mathbf{G}}_t)$, where
$\widetilde{\mathbf{G}}_t$ is an unbiased stochastic gradient with
coordinatewise variance $\operatorname{Var}(\widetilde{\mathbf{G}}_{t,ij}\mid
\mathbf{W}_t)\le\sigma_{ij}^2/n_b$ and $n_b$ the mini-batch size. In that case a
Markov--Jensen argument yields $|\mathbf{g}_{t,ij}|\,q_{ij,t}\le
\sigma_{ij}/\sqrt{n_b}$, hence
$R_T=\mathcal{O}(\|\sigma\|_1/\sqrt{mn\,n_b})$ with
$\|\sigma\|_1=\sum_{i,j}\sigma_{ij}$, which vanishes as $n_b\to\infty$.

This estimate bounds $q_{ij,t}$ by the noise level $\sigma_{ij}/\sqrt{n_b}$ and
is therefore specific to $\operatorname{sign}(\widetilde{\mathbf{G}}_t)$, whose
only source of sign error is gradient noise. It does not extend to the
polar-sign oracle: even with exact gradients ($\sigma_{ij}=0$) the latter
satisfies $q_{ij,t}=\mathbf{1}[\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{polar}
(\mathbf{g}_t))_{ij}\neq\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{g}_{t,ij})]$, which need not
vanish. Consequently the finalized $\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{T})$ guarantee holds for
the gradient-sign update $\mathbf{S}_t=\operatorname{sign}(\widetilde{\mathbf{G}}_t)$,
whose direction never invokes the polar factor, and not for the LMO-based
direction $\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{M}_t))$ to which it
is attributed.

On the instance of Theorem~\ref{th:1} the residual $R_T$ is not merely
unbounded: it exceeds $\mathcal{G}_T$. Consider the linear
objective~\eqref{eq:lin_obj} with the $4\times4$ gradient $\mathbf{G}$ of
Theorem~\ref{th:1}. By Proposition~\ref{prop:reduction} one has
$\mathbf{g}_t\equiv\mathbf{G}$ and
$\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{M}_t)=\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{G})$ at every
iteration, so the oracle is deterministic and the inner-product identity
$\mathbb{E}[\langle\mathbf{g}_t,\mathbf{D}_t\rangle\mid\mathbf{W}_t]=
\tfrac1{\sqrt{mn}}\sum_{ij}|\mathbf{g}_{t,ij}|(1-2q_{ij,t})$ underlying
\eqref{eq:mishra_generic} reduces to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mishra_identity}
\sum_{i,j}|\mathbf{G}_{ij}|\,(1-2q_{ij})
=\bigl\langle \mathbf{G},\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{polar}(\mathbf{G}))\bigr\rangle
=-\tfrac{42468}{103}<0 .
\end{equation}
Therefore $\sum_{ij}|\mathbf{G}_{ij}|\,q_{ij}>\tfrac12\|\mathbf{G}\|_1$, and at
every iteration
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mishra_dominates}
\frac{2}{\sqrt{mn}}\sum_{i,j}|\mathbf{G}_{ij}|\,q_{ij}
\;>\;
\frac{\|\mathbf{G}\|_1}{\sqrt{mn}}
\;=\;\mathcal{G}_t .
\end{equation}
The unestimated term $R_T$ in~\eqref{eq:mishra_generic} thus strictly exceeds
the left-hand side, so the bound reduces to
$\mathcal{G}_T\le(\text{nonnegative})+R_T$ with $R_T>\mathcal{G}_T$ and imposes
no constraint on $\mathcal{G}_T$. In agreement, \eqref{eq:mishra_identity}
yields the exact per-step increase
$f(\mathbf{X}_{t+1})-f(\mathbf{X}_t)=-\eta\langle\mathbf{G},\mathbf{D}_t\rangle
=\tfrac{\eta}{4}\cdot\tfrac{42468}{103}>0$ for every $\eta>0$, which is the
divergence established in Theorem~\ref{th:1}.
