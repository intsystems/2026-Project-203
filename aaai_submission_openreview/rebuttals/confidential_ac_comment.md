Dear AC/SPC, our paper explores how to combine Muon with sign compression. We investigate signing before, after or on both sides of orthogonalization and applying error feedback at different stages. The reviewers value the resulting failure results (Theorems 1–4); their main concerns are novelty beyond EF21-Muon, the theory–practice gap and inconsistent tables. SignMuon itself (Mishra et al.) is a May 2026 preprint developed independently of this work.

For Ueq5 and pqje, the submitted CNN2/CIFAR-10 logs reproduce A8: MuonSign 82.94±0.19% versus SignSGD 81.44±0.15%. We corrected Table 2, its reversed comparison and Table 1.

We completed learning-rate tuning for all 10 methods on federated CNN2/CIFAR-10 with 11 Dirichlet(0.1) clients and 5 final seeds. EF21-MuonSign (84.03%) and EF21-MuonUSign (83.84%) keep their IID accuracy, whereas methods that sign or orthogonalize client messages before aggregation lose 35–45 points: SignMuon falls from 85.72% to 42.96%, and Muon, whose clients orthogonalize before averaging, from 85.98% to 46.02%. Theorem 5 assumes no bound on client dissimilarity. See our response to 7S1b, Q2 for the full table.

We also extended EF21-MuonUSign and EF21-MuonSign convergence to fixed EMA/Nesterov and constant/cosine schedules with proofs verified in Lean, made the counterexamples lower bounded, extended Theorem 4 to decreasing and budget-tuned rates (see our response to Tx34), and proved a sufficient condition for SignMuon convergence.
