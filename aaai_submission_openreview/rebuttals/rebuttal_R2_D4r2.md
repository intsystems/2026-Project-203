It holds when signing retains a uniform positive fraction of the polar update’s alignment with momentum, with joint smoothness and unbiased bounded-variance noise. Layers may interact, and learning rate and momentum are chosen for $T$. This identifies a sufficient condition for SignMuon to converge; measuring it during network training remains open.
