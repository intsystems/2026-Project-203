Thank you for pressing us on novelty and the exact impossibility claim.

1. Re contributions beyond EF21: The new submitted analysis is the explicit ascent constructions and the smooth, lower-bounded output-EF limit cycle. It shows why tracking a polar direction can fail although gradient-side EF succeeds: the polar target can jump by constant order independently of the step size. The base positive rates instantiate EF21-Muon; we will credit that directly.
2. Re Theorem 4: Theorem 4 says $\forall L,\eta>0,\mu\in[0,1),\ \exists f_{L,\eta,\mu}$ causing divergence for the specified output-EF recursion with fixed parameters. It rules out universal constant-step rules based only on $L,\mu$. It does not cover decreasing schedules or rule out horizon-tuned finite-time rates: the objective depends on the parameters, and its gradient floor scales as $L\eta$.
Re ranking and necessity: The theory predicts no empirical ranking. Our added SignMuon analysis gives a first-moment rate under a uniform positive alignment margin, not a predicted winner. Separately, new fixed softplus witnesses prove failure for every positive step schedule and EMA/Nesterov momentum for the three plain placements; this stronger statement does not extend to output EF. We will remove “unrepairable” and “only changing the compressor”: failing a sufficient condition excludes neither other analyses nor other parameter choices.
